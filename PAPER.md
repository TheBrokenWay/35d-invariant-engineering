## 1. Abstract

This paper proposes a way to approach Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE), an autoimmune disease, using the Mount Olympus platform. A target is a position on a 35-dimensional map of molecular state. A candidate is judged on how strongly it might act and on whether it stays stable and safe under a fixed rule set, the Tillar Laws. The method is constraint-first: load the disease's limits, mark zones to avoid, build only candidates that fit.

## 2. Problem: What Drives SLE

SLE is not purely random immune misbehavior. **TREX1** is an enzyme that clears stray DNA from inside cells. When it is missing or faulty, that DNA builds up and triggers two alarms:

- **cGAS-STING**, which drives production of type I interferon (IFN), a signaling protein that rallies the immune system.
- **TLR7/9**, which sustains IFN production and autoimmunity.

This is established in Aicardi-Goutières syndrome and lupus-like conditions (PMID: 18285819, 19838199).

**The theory's framing.** TREX1 failure is a drift from stable structure and energy balance on the 35-dimensional map. Three laws describe it:

- **L8, Propagation Threshold.** A signal should spread only past a set threshold. Runaway IFN is a failure of this.
- **U34, Global Sum.** Energy must balance. Chronic IFN production breaks that balance if left uncontained.
- **U27, Unified Self.** A candidate must be recognized as "self." It must not bind mitochondrial or self-DNA (see also the harm-prohibition laws L4 and L10).

## 3. Method

The platform places molecules and biological states on the 35-dimensional map. A consensus engine and the Tillar Laws judge each candidate. Each must pass these checks:

- **L10, Unit of Value:** harm stays below a set limit.
- **L11, Deterministic Engine:** toxicity stays below a set limit.
- **U27, Unified Self:** no binding to self-DNA; coherent structure.
- **U34, Global Sum:** no energy drift during evaluation.
- **L39, Unified Self Principle:** harm to another is harm to self.

A final governance gate enforces these laws. A candidate whose full safety profile qualifies as approved can clear the gate as a whole.

## 4. Disease Profile

- **Mechanism:** TREX1, then cGAS-STING, then type I IFN.
- **Targets:** BAFF, CD19, TREX1, cGAS-STING, IFNAR.
- **Predictive marker:** type I IFN gene signature (linked to anifrolumab response).
- **Diagnostic markers:** ANA, anti-dsDNA, anti-Sm, C3/C4, aPL, CBC, urinalysis.
